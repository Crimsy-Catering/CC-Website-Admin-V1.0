    FAQs

    Quick answers to common questions.

    How do I get a price?

    Choose a duration in the estimator for a guide price, then send a quote request. We reply by email with an itemised quote.

    Why is a 4 hour booking quoted individually?

    Four hour bookings are priced case by case. Send us your details and we will come back with a price.

    Do you travel to my venue?

    Yes, we serve events nationwide. Travel is charged separately and shown clearly in your quote.

    What events do you cater for?

    Weddings, birthdays, corporate events and other celebrations.

    What about allergies and dietary needs?

    Please tell us about any allergies or dietary requirements in your enquiry. We will talk it through with you and confirm what we can safely provide before anything is booked. We cannot promise to accommodate every requirement.

    Is my enquiry a confirmed booking?

    No. An enquiry is a request. A date is only confirmed once we have agreed it with you.
